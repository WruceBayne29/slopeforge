# Code of Conduct

SlopeForge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to this project. This page is a short summary.

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, as a contributor or a maintainer, pledges to make
participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind. Assume good intentions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Review the work, not the person.
- Be patient with beginners. Many people here are students.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and code review, and
anywhere a person is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer through
the contact details on their GitHub profile: https://github.com/WruceBayne29. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor
for behaviour they consider inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
